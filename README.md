☕ Coffee Shop Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI
An interactive Business Intelligence dashboard developed using Microsoft Power BI to analyse coffee shop sales performance, identify revenue trends, evaluate product performance, and generate actionable business insights.

📌 Project Overview
The objective of this project is to transform coffee shop transactional sales data into meaningful business insights through interactive visualisations and KPI monitoring.

The dashboard provides an overview of sales performance across different product categories, individual products, store locations, weekdays, weekends, and hours of the day.

It is designed to help business stakeholders monitor performance, understand purchasing patterns, and make data-driven decisions related to inventory planning, product promotions, and operational efficiency.

🎯 Business Objectives
Monitor overall business performance using key performance indicators (KPIs).

Analyse monthly sales trends and month-on-month performance.

Identify high-performing product categories and individual products.

Compare revenue contribution across store locations.

Evaluate weekday versus weekend sales patterns.

Analyse sales distribution across different days and hours.

Generate business recommendations based on observed sales trends.

🛠️ Tools & Technologies
Microsoft Power BI – Dashboard development and interactive visualisation.

Data Analysis – Sales performance evaluation and trend analysis.

Business Intelligence – KPI monitoring and business insight generation.

💡 Key Business Insights
Product Category Performance: Coffee is the largest revenue-generating category, followed by tea.

Product Performance: Barista Espresso is the leading individual product by sales.

Store Performance: Hell's Kitchen records the highest sales among the three locations, although the differences are relatively small.

Weekday vs Weekend: Weekday sales contribute a larger share of total revenue than weekend sales.

Time-Based Patterns: Hourly sales analysis provides an opportunity to understand demand patterns and improve operational planning.

🚀 Business Recommendations
Based on the observed sales patterns, the following recommendations could be considered:

Inventory Optimisation: Prioritise stock availability for high-revenue categories such as coffee and tea.

Promotional Strategies: Explore targeted weekend promotions to improve weekend sales.

Workforce Planning: Use hourly sales patterns to align staffing levels with customer demand.

Product Strategy: Evaluate top-performing products for promotional campaigns and menu planning.

Store Performance Monitoring: Compare store-level performance regularly to identify operational improvement opportunities.
